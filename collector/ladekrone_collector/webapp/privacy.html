Privacy

In short: Ladekrone has no accounts, no cookies, no tracking and no advertising. Your location stays on your device. Deutsche Fassung

Who is behind Ladekrone

Ladekrone is a private, non-commercial open-source project by uekala. Questions and feedback are welcome on GitHub.

Your location

When you tap “Use my location”, your browser asks for permission. The location is used only on your device to calculate distances and prices. It is neither stored nor sent to us or to third parties. The app loads price data for whole areas of roughly 25 by 25 kilometres; your exact location cannot be derived from that.

Place search

The search for a town or postcode runs on your device, using a list of places that the app loads once. What you type is not sent to any search service.

Storage on your device

The app keeps your filters, the last place you searched, the places you save, a monthly fee you enter and your language choice in your browser's storage. So that it also works offline, it stores the app files and the price data loaded last. All of this stays on your device. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Sharing

“Share” creates a link with the place you searched and your filters. The coordinates in it are rounded to about 100 metres. The link is created on your device; where you send it is up to you.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

The app and the price data are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you load them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and to protect it against abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is providing the app. Details are in the GitHub privacy statement.

Map

The map only appears when you tap “Map”. Your browser then loads the map data from OpenFreeMap, a free map service without accounts or cookies. For technical reasons OpenFreeMap learns your IP address and the map area you are viewing. Without the map the app works as before, and no data goes to OpenFreeMap.

Start route

“Start route” opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with the destination. From then on, the privacy terms of that map service apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As Ladekrone itself stores no personal data, this mainly concerns the processing by GitHub.
